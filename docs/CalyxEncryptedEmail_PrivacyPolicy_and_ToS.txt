CALYX ENCRYPTED EMAIL (com.calyxlabs.mail • calyxmail.org • mail.calyxlabsinc.org)
PRIVACY POLICY & TERMS OF SERVICE
Effective Date: November 19, 2026
Developer / Publisher: Tristen Martinez — Calyx Labs Inc.

1. ZERO-KNOWLEDGE OPENQPEP ENCRYPTION (INTERNAL @CALYXMAIL.ORG MAIL)
All emails exchanged between @calyxmail.org users are encrypted client-side using OpenQPEP Production Candidate 1.2.0 before transmission. Private .qpkrf key rings remain strictly locked on your device under your QPKD-Argon2id passphrase and are never transmitted to Calyx Labs Inc. servers.

2. STANDARD EMAIL INTEROPERABILITY (3RD-PARTY PROVIDERS)
Emails sent to or received from external 3rd-party domains (such as gmail.com or yahoo.com) are transmitted using standard RFC 5322 / SMTP / TLS protocols with DKIM signing to ensure universal deliverability. Standard emails are not end-to-end encrypted with OpenQPEP.

3. ACCEPTABLE USE & ANTI-SPAM POLICY
Users of @calyxmail.org agree not to send unsolicited bulk email (spam), phishing, or malware. Accounts violating anti-abuse policies may be suspended to protect calyxmail.org domain reputation and deliverability.
