1. ZERO TELEMETRY & SOVEREIGN END-TO-END ENCRYPTION
Calyx Encrypted Messenger encrypts all messages and files on-device using OpenQPEP Production Candidate 1.1.0 (Signature QLX-4096 + QVS-256 | HGC-448 | CC-512 | qSponge-512) before transmission. Private .qpkrf keys remain strictly locked on your device under your mandatory QPKD-Argon passphrase and are never transmitted to any server. Camera access is used exclusively on-device for the in-app Scannable CLX-Code (SC Code) scanner.
